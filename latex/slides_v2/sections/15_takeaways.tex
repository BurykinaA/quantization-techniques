    \begin{column}{0.6\textwidth}
      \footnotesize
      \begin{enumerate}
        \setlength\itemsep{0.4em}
        \item \textbf{ADC $\ne$ conventional quantisation.} Fixed $\delta$, fixed clip, no per-layer knobs. Algorithmic fixes must act on $x,w$ statistics.
        \item \textbf{LLM outliers make a single $k$ infeasible.} Diagonals + rotations solve this at the projection level.
        \item \textbf{Block-wise + $\alpha$-mix is the biggest PTQ lever.} Same knob halves INT8 ADC PPL and enables INT4 recovery.
        \item \textbf{Staged training.} MLP-first warm-up makes INT4 PTQ trainable at all.
        \item \textbf{Post-ADC LoRA breaks the PTQ plateau.} 0.22\% extra params $\to$ INT4 matches INT8.
      \end{enumerate}
    \end{column}
