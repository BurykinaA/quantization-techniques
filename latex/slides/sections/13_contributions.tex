    \begin{column}{0.54\textwidth}
      \begin{enumerate}
        \setlength\itemsep{0.25em}
        \item \textbf{Dead zone was a training problem, not hardware.}\\
              Better FlatQuant calibration: 81\% $\to$ 10.3\% dead rate.

        \item \textbf{Propagated calibration fixes INT8.}\\
              ADC PPL: 28.86 $\to$ 14.41 ($-47\%$), near FP baseline.

        \item \textbf{Staged PTQ closes the INT4 gap to $\times$1.4.}\\
              $\alpha$-mixed loss + MLP-first diagonal training.

        \item \textbf{MLP drives bypass; attn drives ADC robustness.}\\
              Staged training combines both — insight, not just tuning.

        \item \textbf{Post-ADC LoRA breaks the PTQ plateau.}\\
              INT4 ADC 13.96 $<$ INT8 PTQ 14.41.
              Generalises to C4 ($-50\%$ vs PTQ baseline).
      \end{enumerate}

      \vspace{0.3em}
      \begin{center}
        \small\textit{The right place to correct ADC error is
        \textbf{after} ADC, not before it.}
      \end{center}
    \end{column}
