  \begin{columns}[T]
    \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
      \footnotesize
      \textbf{What is quantisation?} Replace FP tensors with low-bit integers:
      \[
        \hat x \;=\; s\cdot \mathrm{round}(x/s),\quad s = \tfrac{\max|x|}{2^{b-1}{-}1}
      \]
      The error $\hat x - x$ is what downstream layers see.

      \vspace{0.5em}
      \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.28\linewidth} p{0.65\linewidth}@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{PTQ} & Freeze weights, calibrate on a handful of samples. \emph{Fast} (minutes), \emph{limited} accuracy. \\
        \midrule
        \textbf{QAT} & Full SGD with fake-quant in the graph. \emph{Accurate} but costs multiple epochs of training. \\
        \bottomrule
      \end{tabular}
    \end{column}

    % ── right column: rotation lineage ─────────────────────────────

      \vspace{0.3em}
      \textbf{Why FlatQuant for our work:}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item \emph{trainable} — we can add objectives (ADC-aware);
        \item \emph{Kronecker} ($T = T_1 \otimes T_2$) — memory \& compute $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt d)$;
        \item per-projection rotations fold naturally into block-wise calibration.
      \end{itemize}
